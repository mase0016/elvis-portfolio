## Project Overview

Kawempe Decorous Nursery & Primary School is a real school in Kampala, Uganda, offering education from Nursery through Primary Seven. They needed a website that could clearly present their programs, admissions process, and school life to parents, while being simple for staff to maintain going forward. I was brought on to build the site from the ground up.

## Key Features

- **Clear program structure**: Nursery-to-Primary-Seven offerings, curriculum, and admissions info organized into dedicated pages so parents can find what they need without calling the school.
- **Admissions-focused CTAs**: Prominent "Contact Us" and enrollment prompts throughout, since admissions run year-round.
- **Photo-driven storytelling**: A gallery section showcasing classroom learning, school facilities, and daily life to build trust with prospective families.
- **Mobile-friendly layout**: Built to work smoothly for parents browsing on phones, which make up the majority of the school's traffic.

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5**: Semantic page structure across all sections.
- **CSS3**: Custom styling for layout, spacing, and responsive behavior.
- **JavaScript**: Navigation and small interactive UI behaviors.

## Key Sections

1. **Home**: Hero introduction, head teacher's welcome message, and key differentiators (merit-based admissions, curriculum, educators, holistic development).
2. **About Us**: School mission and "Who We Are" overview.
3. **Academics & Admissions**: Curriculum details and enrollment information.
4. **Gallery**: Photos of classroom learning and school facilities.
5. **Contact Us**: School location, phone, and email for inquiries.
