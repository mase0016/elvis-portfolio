## Project Overview

This is my personal portfolio site, and the one you're looking at right now. It showcases my UX/UI and front-end work, ties my case studies together in one place, and gives employers a direct way to reach me.

## Features

- **Dynamic Project Pages**: Each case study is written in MDX and rendered as its own page, so adding a new project is just a new file.
- **Interactive Prototype Embed**: A custom `PhoneEmbed` component embeds live, fully interactive prototypes inside an iPhone-style frame, so reviewers can tap through the real app right on the page.
- **Responsive Design**: Built mobile-first and tuned across breakpoints, from phones to wide monitors.
- **Contact Form**: A working contact form that emails submissions directly to my inbox.
- **Smooth Animations**: GSAP-driven scroll reveals and page transitions throughout.
- **Automated Screenshots**: Project images are captured and kept current with Playwright.

- **Tailwind CSS**: For rapid UI development and styling.
